How this works
  1. You and your partner each see different letters of the same hidden word.
  2. During a round you may communicate only through the game's icons, saved sigils, replies, and its own signals — no talking, typing, gestures, screen-sharing, or naming/spelling letters. Count icons and shown positions are fair game.
  3. Send whole message cards into one shared conversation. Save any useful sequence you both approve as a numbered sigil you can reuse for the rest of the session.
  4. When you think you know the word, enter it privately. Only a scrambled fingerprint of your guess is sent — never the letters. You solve it when both fingerprints match and the word is right.

Concealment here is casual, not bullet-proof: this is a static site with no server, so a determined partner could work around it. It's built for players who want to actually play.

Rooms use PeerJS for connection setup and may use Google STUN or Metered's Open Relay TURN service. Those third parties can receive connection metadata; gameplay traffic is peer-to-peer when a direct connection succeeds.
