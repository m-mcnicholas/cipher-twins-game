How to play (30 seconds)
  1. You and your partner each see different letters of the same hidden word, rendered in your own private glyph font — you cannot read your partner's glyphs, and they cannot read yours.
  2. Together you hold every letter, but neither of you holds enough to solve it alone.
  3. Describe your glyphs to each other using only the shared icon palette — shape, position, count, category. No typing, no letters, no numbers.
  4. Drop icons onto the shared message board. Your partner sees them appear in real time.
  5. When you think you know the word, spell your guess with the letter tiles. When you both agree and you're right, you advance.
